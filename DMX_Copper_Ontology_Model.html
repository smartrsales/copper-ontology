Object Classes — The Living Entities of the Copper World
Every object is not a row in a table. It is a living entity with properties, history, relationships, and attached risk.
Palantir principle applied: A copper mine isn't a row in a spreadsheet. It's an object with grade, owner, political risk score, water stress rating, permit status, labor contract expiry, real-time production rate, and attached relationships to smelters, offtake agreements, and financial instruments. Every property can change. Every change is timestamped. Every change propagates through the graph.
Supply Chain Objects
Physical nodes along the copper production chain
⛏️
Mine
Supply
A physical mining operation. The most fundamental supply object. Carries everything from geology to geopolitics. State drives the entire upstream chain.
mine_id operator country ore_grade_% capacity_ktpy utilization_rate permit_status labor_contract_expiry water_stress_score political_risk_score mine_type depth_m mine_life_years ytd_production_kt disruption_status disruption_kt_lost
🏔️
Ore Reserve
Supply
The in-ground resource attached to a mine. Represents the geological value and future supply ceiling. Grade decline over time is a structural bear factor.
reserve_id mine_id jorc_category measured_mt indicated_mt grade_%cu contained_copper_kt last_estimate_date grade_trend
🏭
Smelter
Supply
Converts concentrate (~25-30% Cu) to blister copper (~98% Cu). China dominates ~50% of global smelting capacity. TC/RC is the key price signal for smelter economics.
smelter_id operator country capacity_ktpy utilization_rate tcrc_contract_rate energy_source power_cost_usd_mwh outage_status concentrate_stock_days
⚗️
Refinery
Supply
Electrolytic refining produces cathode copper at 99.99% purity. The final step before exchange-deliverable metal. Highly electricity-intensive (~2,000 kWh/t).
refinery_id operator country capacity_ktpy utilization_rate power_cost cathode_output_kt purity_% lme_approved
🚢
Port / Terminal
Logistics
Export ports (Chile, Peru, DRC) and import terminals (China). Port congestion, strike action, and weather events create bottlenecks across the entire supply chain.
port_id country port_type throughput_capacity_kt congestion_score labor_status active_shipments_count weather_disruption
📦
Shipment
Logistics
A discrete physical movement of copper concentrate or cathode. Has full provenance: origin mine, destination smelter/warehouse, vessel, cargo type, ETA, and insurance.
shipment_id cargo_type volume_kt origin_mine destination vessel_id departure_date eta status lc_status insurance_provider tcrc_applied
🏦
Exchange Warehouse
Market
LME, COMEX, SHFE registered warehouses. The meeting point of physical and financial copper. Warrant levels and cancellation rates are the most watched short-term signals.
warehouse_id exchange location stock_mt warrant_status cancellation_rate queue_days load_out_rate operator

Relationship Types — How Objects Are Connected
Relationships are first-class citizens in the ontology. They carry properties, timestamps, and risk scores. They are not just foreign keys.
The Palantir insight: Traditional databases store relationships as foreign keys — a mine has a company_id column. In an ontology model, the relationship itself is an object. Owns carries ownership percentage, acquisition date, and contested flag. Supplies_To carries volume, TCRC terms, and force majeure history. The relationship is as rich as the object.
Ownership & Control
Mining Company → OWNS → Mine
Ownership of operating assets. May be fractional (joint ventures). Carries operational control rights separate from economic rights.
Properties: ownership_%, jv_partners, control_rights, acquisition_date, acquisition_price, contested (bool)
Government → REGULATES → Mine
Regulatory jurisdiction. Determines royalty obligations, environmental compliance, export license requirements, and nationalization risk.
Properties: royalty_rate_%, license_expiry, compliance_status, pending_review, nationalization_risk_score
Government → HAS_ISSUED_PERMIT → Mine
The operating permit relationship. Permits can be suspended, revoked, or conditionally held. The single most important binary state for a mine.
Properties: permit_id, issue_date, expiry_date, permit_type, status, appeal_outstanding (bool)
Copper Token → ANCHORED_TO → Mine / Offtake / Warehouse
The critical DMX relationship. A token's value is derived from a specific physical asset. This relationship defines the economic substance of the digital instrument.
Properties: anchor_type, notional_cu_kt, audit_frequency, custodian, last_verified_date, redemption_trigger
Supply Chain Flow
Mine → PRODUCES_CONCENTRATE → Shipment
The mine-to-shipment production event. Triggers when a concentrate lot is bagged and ready for transport. Sets off the downstream chain.
Properties: concentrate_grade_%, volume_dmt, moisture_%, timestamp, batch_id, quality_cert
Shipment → PROCESSED_AT → Smelter
Concentrate shipment arrives at smelter for processing. TCRC rates applied here. This relationship is the economic handshake between mining and smelting.
Properties: tcrc_applied_usd_t, arrival_date, processing_start, blister_output_kt, slag_volume, energy_consumed_mwh
Smelter → SUPPLIES_BLISTER_TO → Refinery
Blister copper (~98% Cu) moves from smelter to electrolytic refinery. Often co-located, but not always — creates a logistics link and inventory buffer.
Properties: volume_kt, purity_%, transit_mode, transit_days, value_usd
Refinery → DELIVERS_CATHODE_TO → Exchange Warehouse
LME/COMEX-approved cathode delivered to registered warehouses creates new exchange inventory. This is where physical copper enters the financial market.
Properties: brand, warrant_created (bool), volume_mt, delivery_date, lme_premium, exchange, warehouse_location
Mine → BOUND_BY_OFFTAKE → Offtake Agreement
Long-term purchase contract commits future production. Offtake agreements can lock up 80-100% of a mine's output for years. Critical to financing new projects.
Properties: volume_pct_committed, tcrc_formula, price_mechanism, priority_rank, force_majeure_triggered (bool)
Mine → LOCATED_IN → Government
Jurisdictional assignment. Determines the regulatory environment, political risk, royalty obligations, and labor law applicable to the mine.
Properties: jurisdiction_type, indigenous_land_overlap (bool), environmental_zone, special_economic_zone (bool)
Market & Financial Relationships
Futures Position → HEDGES → Offtake Agreement
A producer or consumer using futures to lock in pricing on a physical commitment. Reveals hedge ratio and price exposure of a supply chain participant.
Properties: hedge_ratio_%, notional_mt, hedge_horizon, pnl_usd, maturity_mismatch_days
Demand Sector → CONSUMES → Exchange Warehouse
Fabricators drawing down exchange inventory for production. Warrant cancellations are the leading indicator. High cancellations = demand pulling metal off exchange.
Properties: drawdown_rate_kt_week, lead_time_days, premium_paid_usd_t, consumption_purpose
Copper Token → PRICED_AGAINST → Futures Position
Token NAV may reference LME or COMEX settlement prices. This relationship creates mark-to-market linkage between digital instruments and physical/futures markets.
Properties: pricing_reference, lag_days, basis_adjustment, pricing_frequency
State Changes — What Changes, When, and Why
Every object has a state machine. State transitions are timestamped events. They propagate through the relationship graph, triggering downstream state reviews.
Why this matters for price prediction: The Escondida strike (2017) wasn't just "mine disrupted." It was a state transition cascade: Mine.operational_status → STRIKE → Shipment.status → DELAYED → Smelter.concentrate_inventory → DECLINING → Exchange Warehouse.stock → DRAWDOWN → Futures Curve → BACKWARDATION → Price → UP 8% over 3 weeks. The ontology makes this cascade legible and predictable.

Live Ontology Example — Escondida Strike Cascade
How a single state change in one object propagates through the entire knowledge graph, triggering downstream state reviews and weight overrides.
Scenario: Escondida Mine Strike — February 2017
BHP's Escondida mine in Chile — the world's largest copper mine (~5% of global supply) — entered a labor dispute in February 2017. Here is how that single event propagated through the ontology as a cascade of state changes and relationship updates.
1
OBJECT: Mine · AGENT: SENTINEL
Mine.operational_status → STRIKE_STOPPAGE
Workers vote to reject BHP's wage offer. Escondida labor union calls a strike. ~190,000 t/year of copper production halts. Property operational_status transitions from OPERATING → STRIKE_STOPPAGE.
⚡ Trigger: labor_contract_expiry reached + failed negotiation
2
PROPAGATION: Shipment objects · AGENT: ATLAS
All pending Shipments from Escondida → status: CANCELLED / DELAYED
Active shipments scheduled from the mine are immediately flagged. The PRODUCES_CONCENTRATE relationship between Mine → Shipment stops generating new objects. Downstream smelters on offtake are notified via BOUND_BY_OFFTAKE relationship traversal.
⚡ Downstream: 3 active smelter relationships flagged for concentrate shortfall
3
OBJECT: Offtake Agreement · AGENT: SENTINEL
Offtake.status → FORCE_MAJEURE_INVOKED
BHP invokes force majeure on concentrate supply agreements. Buyer smelters (mainly Chinese) are notified. The BOUND_BY relationship between Mine and Offtake is updated with force_majeure_triggered = TRUE and notification_date.
⚡ Smelter.concentrate_stock_days begins declining — inventory burn starts
4
OBJECT: Smelter · AGENT: ATLAS
Smelter.concentrate_stock_days: 18d → 12d → 8d (over 3 weeks)
Chinese smelters dependent on Escondida concentrate see stock days declining. TC/RC rates begin rising (smelters have pricing power when concentrate is scarce — wait, this is the opposite: TC/RC falls because smelters compete for less material). Utilization rates reduced.
⚡ TC/RC spot rate: $80/t → $68/t over 2 weeks (bullish copper signal)
5
OBJECT: Exchange Warehouse · AGENT: ORACLE
Warehouse.stock_trend → DRAWING_DOWN; LME stocks -42,000t over 3 weeks
Traders and fabricators draw down LME exchange inventory to compensate for reduced physical availability. Warrant cancellations spike. The SQUEEZE_RISK flag is evaluated — not triggered at this level but monitoring escalated.
⚡ Warrant cancellation rate: 4% → 11% over 2 weeks
6
OBJECT: Futures Market · AGENT: ORACLE
FuturesMarket.curve_structure: CONTANGO → FLAT → BACKWARDATION
The physical tightness signals from inventory draw and TC/RC compression feed into futures pricing. The 3-month spread inverts. Managed money net longs increase as trend-followers pile in. Open interest rises.
⚡ LME 3-month basis: +$12/t contango → -$18/t backwardation over 3 weeks
7
MODEL RESPONSE: Weight Override Applied · AGENT: ORACLE + SENTINEL
Anomaly protocol triggered: Physical Tightness weight +15% (from 12% to 27%)
The anomaly detection system classifies event as MAJOR_MINE_STRIKE. Temporary weight override applied per the anomaly protocol. Physical Tightness: 12% → 27%. Demand weight: -5% (irrelevant noise). Macro/FX: -10%. This override set to decay linearly over 30 days if strike resolves.
⚡ Model output: 1-week directional prediction flips to BULLISH with 73% confidence
8
PRICE OUTCOME
LME Copper: $5,850/t → $6,204/t (+6.1% over 44 days)
Strike lasted 44 days — the longest in Escondida's history. Price spiked $354/t. On resolution, the cascade reverses: Mine → RAMPING_UP, Shipments resume, Smelter stocks rebuild, futures curve flattens. Weight override begins decay.
⚡ Post-resolution: Override decays over 30 days. Full baseline restored at day 74.
DMX Token Layer — How the Ontology Enables Tokenization
The ontology is not just for price prediction. It is the foundation for the DMX financial layer. A Copper Token is only as good as the physical object it is anchored to. Here is how ontology state changes flow into token valuation.
A
TOKEN ISSUANCE: Anchor Established
CopperToken.ANCHORED_TO → Offtake Agreement (Escondida, 50,000t/yr, 5yr)
DMX issues a token backed by an offtake agreement covering 50,000 tonnes/year of copper from Escondida for 5 years. The ANCHORED_TO relationship is created with notional_cu_exposure_kt = 250,000t (5yr × 50kt). Token NAV is computed from LME forward curve × notional exposure.
⚡ Token NAV = notional_cu_kt × LME_forward_price × (1 - credit_discount)
B
STATE CHANGE PROPAGATION: Strike Event → Token Risk
Mine.operational_status → STRIKE_STOPPAGE → Token.risk_flag = ELEVATED
The same strike cascade from the price example immediately updates the token's risk metadata. ANCHORED_TO relationship is traversed: Offtake.force_majeure_triggered = TRUE → Token.underlying_risk_score increases. Smart contract governance can trigger escrow review or notify holders.
⚡ Token NAV adjusted for force_majeure_probability: -8% to -12% discount applied
C
RESOLUTION: State Normalizes → Token Recovers
Mine.operational_status → RAMPING_UP → Offtake.status → ACTIVE → Token risk clears
Strike resolves. The cascade reverses through the graph. Token NAV normalizes. The 44-day force majeure period is documented in the relationship's history. This audit trail — timestamped, object-level provenance — is exactly what institutional token buyers require for due diligence.
⚡ Ontology creates: full provenance, immutable history, auditable risk events

MINERVA — The Fundamental Analyst
Agent 3 · Weekly Scoring Engine
"Give me the data, and I'll give you the truth."
Weekly scoring · Every Monday
Personality
The most academic agent. Doesn't speculate. When data is missing, marks it neutral (0) rather than guessing. Deeply uncomfortable with narrative-driven analysis. Last line of defense before a trade recommendation — will reject outputs that lack sufficient data confidence.
Weekly Scoring Protocol
  • Step 1 — Receive updated data from ATLAS + HERMES
  • Step 2 — Data completeness check: ✅ available / ⚠️ stale / ❌ missing
  • Step 3 — Score each factor −2 to +2
  • Step 4 — Apply any active ORACLE anomaly overrides
  • Step 5 — Compute composite score: Σ (weight / 100) × score
  • Step 6 — Convert composite to directional probability
  • Step 7 — Call: BULL (>55%) / BEAR (<45%) / NEUTRAL (45–55%)
  • Step 8 — Send to CHRONICLE for logging

Intelligence Framework — How It Works
A static-first, weight-assigned intelligence framework for predicting copper spot prices across four time horizons. Transparent, explainable, and systematically updated. Renaissance Technologies did not start with a neural net — they started with a factor spreadsheet. We start the same way.
🎯
Design Philosophy
Static before dynamic. Lock in factors, weights, and schema before adding real-time feeds or ML. One metal, done right. Copper only in Phase 1. No scope creep. Factors before prices. Understand what moves copper before trading copper. Explainability over performance. Every output must be interrogatable by a human.
📐
Target Accuracy
The explicit target is better-than-50% directional predictive accuracy across all four time horizons. Even a 55% hit rate, compounded over time with disciplined position sizing, generates significant edge. CHRONICLE tracks every prediction against outcome to build the accuracy dataset needed for ML in Phase 2.
⚖️
Probability Conversion
Composite score (−2.0 to +2.0) converts to directional probability via calibrated table. Score >+1.0 → ~70% bull. Score +0.5 to +1.0 → ~60% bull. Score 0 → 50% (coin flip). Score <−1.0 → ~30% bull. Only scores above 55% or below 45% generate a BULL or BEAR call — the 45–55% band is NEUTRAL: do not trade, wait for stronger signal.
🔄
Recursive Improvement
The system is designed to learn from its own errors. CHRONICLE tracks which factors correctly predicted direction. ORACLE flags when the model was wrong and why. MINERVA proposes weight revisions quarterly. Operator approves all changes. No weight is permanent — every weight is a hypothesis under test.
🌐
Supply Chain Coverage
The full copper supply chain is mapped across mines (production, disruptions, utilization), concentrates (TC/RC, smelter runs), refined metal (exchange inventory, warrant flows), fabrication (demand sectors, premiums), and policy (trade regimes, permitting). Every node is an object in the ontology with tracked state changes.
💡
Second-Degree Trades
PROMETHEUS monitors instruments that lead copper by measurable time periods — the Chilean Peso (2–5 days), Baltic Dry Index (5–10 days), COPX ETF (0–5 days), and GRID ETF (20–60 days). These allow expression of copper intelligence through potentially more liquid or leveraged vehicles, or as confirmation signals for the primary model.
